# Practical 3: Privacy Impact Assessment (PIA) of Instagram

## Aim

To conduct a Privacy Impact Assessment (PIA) of Instagram and identify potential privacy risks associated with the collection, processing, storage, and sharing of user data, along with suitable measures to mitigate them.

### Technology/System Selected

#### System: Instagram Social Media Platform

#### Organization: Instagram (Meta Platforms)

#### Website: Instagram

#### Purpose

Instagram is a social media platform that allows users to create profiles, share photos and videos, communicate through messages, follow other users, view content, and interact with advertisements and other services.

### Requirements

- Internet connection
- Web browser or smartphone
- Instagram application
- Instagram account (optional)
- Meta Privacy Policy

### Procedure

- Visit the Instagram website or open the Instagram application.
- Review Instagram/Meta's Privacy Policy.
- Identify the types of personal information collected.
- Examine how user information is processed and used.
- Identify the parties that may receive or process user information.
- Examine cookies and tracking technologies.
- Review Instagram's privacy and security controls.
- Identify potential privacy risks.
- Assess the likelihood and impact of each risk.
- Suggest suitable measures to mitigate the identified risks.

### Data Collected

- Name and username
- Email address and phone number
- Profile information
- Photos and videos
- Comments, likes and other interactions
- Direct messages
- Device information
- IP address
- Location-related information
- Information about accounts and content users interact with
- Information about how users use Instagram
- Advertising and interaction data

### Data Processing Activities

- Users create and manage Instagram accounts.
- Personal information is collected during account creation and profile management.
- Photos, videos, comments and other content are uploaded and stored.
- Instagram records interactions such as likes, follows, searches and comments.
- The platform uses activity and interaction information to personalize content.
- Information may be used to provide and personalize advertisements.
- Cookies and similar technologies may be used for functionality, analytics and advertising.
- Information may be processed by Meta companies, service providers and other partners.
- Security systems process information to detect suspicious activity and protect accounts.

### Privacy Risk Assessment

| Privacy Aspect | Observation | Risk | Impact |
|---|---|---|---|
| Personal Data Collection | Instagram collects various personal, device and activity-related information. | High | High |
| Public Profile | Public profiles may expose usernames, photos, videos and other information to other users. | Medium | High |
| Location Information | Location-related information may be collected or shared through certain features and user activity. | Medium | High |
| Direct Messages | Private messages may contain sensitive personal information. | High | High |
| Data Tracking | User activity may be tracked to personalize content and advertising. | High | High |
| Third-party Sharing | Information may be processed or shared with service providers, partners and other third parties. | Medium | High |
| Data Breach | A security breach could expose personal information and user content. | High | High |
| Account Security | Weak passwords or compromised accounts can result in unauthorized access. | High | High |
| Advertising | User information may be used to personalize advertisements. | Medium | Medium |
| Data Retention | Information may remain stored according to applicable policies and requirements. | Medium | Medium |
| User Control | Users have privacy and security controls, but may not always understand or use them effectively. | Medium | Medium |

### Privacy Risks Identified

- Large amounts of personal and behavioral information may be collected.
- Public profiles and posts may expose personal information to other users.
- Photos and videos may unintentionally reveal sensitive information such as location, identity or daily activities.
- Direct messages may contain sensitive or confidential information.
- Cookies and similar technologies can be used for tracking and advertising purposes.
- Device and activity information can be used to build detailed user profiles.
- Third-party services may process some user information.
- A data breach could expose personal information, photos, videos or other account data.
- Compromised accounts can result in unauthorized access to private content.
- Users may unknowingly share more information than necessary through public posts and profile settings.

### Privacy Mitigation Measures

- Set the Instagram account to private when appropriate.
- Avoid sharing sensitive personal information in posts, stories and biographies.
- Avoid publicly sharing home addresses, phone numbers, passwords or financial information.
- Review privacy settings regularly.
- Enable two-factor authentication.
- Use a strong and unique password.
- Review login activity and remove unknown devices.
- Limit unnecessary application permissions.
- Disable location access when it is not required.
- Be careful when accepting follow requests from unknown users.
- Review connected accounts and third-party applications.
- Avoid sending sensitive personal information through Direct Messages.
- Review advertising and data-related privacy settings.
- Use Instagram's available privacy and security tools.
- Keep the Instagram application and device operating system updated.
- Report suspicious accounts, messages and activities.
- Delete content that is no longer necessary.
- Review Meta's Privacy Policy when major features or policies change.

### Privacy-by-Design Measures

Instagram should follow privacy-by-design and privacy-by-default principles.

- **Data Minimization:** Collect and process only information necessary for providing the service.
- **Purpose Limitation:** Use collected information for clearly defined purposes.
- **Transparency:** Clearly explain how user information is collected, used and shared.
- **Privacy by Default:** Provide privacy-protective settings for users by default where appropriate.
- **Security:** Protect personal information using appropriate technical and organizational security measures.
- **User Control:** Allow users to manage account visibility, content and privacy settings.
- **Access Control:** Restrict access to personal information to authorized personnel and systems.
- **Retention Limitation:** Retain information only for as long as necessary or legally required.
- **Accountability:** Maintain appropriate privacy policies, controls and procedures.

### Risk Rating

| Risk Level | Description | Recommended Action |
|---|---|---|
| Low | Limited privacy impact | Monitor periodically |
| Medium | Moderate privacy impact | Apply additional privacy controls |
| High | Significant privacy impact | Immediate mitigation required |

### Result

The Privacy Impact Assessment of Instagram was successfully performed. The assessment identified potential privacy risks related to personal data collection, public profiles, location information, Direct Messages, tracking technologies, targeted advertising, third-party services, account security and data breaches.

### Conclusion

Instagram provides users with various privacy and security controls, but the platform processes a large amount of personal, behavioral and device-related information. This creates privacy risks related to tracking, public information exposure, targeted advertising, third-party processing and unauthorized access.

Users should regularly review their privacy settings, use strong authentication, limit the information they share publicly, control application permissions and avoid sharing sensitive information through the platform. Appropriate privacy-by-design principles, transparency, data minimization and strong security measures are important for reducing the privacy risks associated with Instagram.
